# Product brief

## Request
    Follow-up: take the Expenses tab live through the ike Odoo layer. Staff can record a bank-transfer expense from their phone in under 30 seconds, and the accountant sees it in Odoo without asking in WhatsApp. Context: MRH Investment (Maldives, shop + warehouse + wholesale, MVR). Different staff pay different expenses and the accountant asks in a WhatsApp group for each amount. Odoo (mrh-investment.odoo.com, saas-19.4) holds expenses as hr.expense, but 31 of 36 records have no receipt, 20 of 36 use the generic category 'Expenses', and some sit in draft, so totals are wrong. Staff have no Odoo logins and will not use Odoo screens. Jobs: Staff - record the transfer I just made, fast, on my phone, with the receipt if I have it. Accountant - see what was spent, by category and day, without asking. Owner/manager - keep bad entries out of the books (a person submits and approves drafts in Odoo).

## Outcomes
-     The Worker is named and shaped as the ike Odoo layer: Worker 'odoo', D1 database 'ike-odoo', and every expense route under /expenses/, so later Odoo areas (quotations, products) get their own prefix without a clash.
-     Staff record one real bank-transfer expense from a phone in under 30 seconds, from opening the Expenses tab to seeing the entry in the day's list as saved.
-     The accountant finds that expense in the real Odoo as a draft hr.expense with the right category, amount and receipt, without asking in WhatsApp.
-     After a person approves it in Odoo, its chip shows 'Approved' within one schedule interval.
-     The owner changes the PIN once in the Cloudflare dashboard; the new PIN saves and the old PIN is refused.

## Scope

### In scope
-     Renaming the Worker to 'odoo' and its D1 database to 'ike-odoo' (worker/wrangler.json), and moving the expense routes under /expenses/ in the Worker and in the page; V2-V5 behaviour unchanged.
-     Read-only checks of the live Odoo schema for the facts the fake Odoo assumed, and corrections where a guess is wrong.
-     Deploy on Cloudflare in the owner's account (workers.dev subdomain 'ike-mrh'; 'ike' was taken), the service URL in the page, one live entry with the owner, and one PIN change by the owner.

### Out of scope
Applicability: applicable
Reason:     The request is about recording bank-transfer expenses and making them visible in Odoo; these items are separate jobs or later decisions.
-     Cash expenses, cash/transfer choice, till reconciliation.
-     Editing or deleting an entry from the page.
-     Submitting, approving, posting or paying from the page or the Worker.
-     A receipt requirement above an amount (decision 6: no limit now; revisit later).
-     Per-person PINs or any staff identity; per-device entry lists.
-     WhatsApp bots, multi-currency, reports beyond the existing dashboard.
-     Changes to ike-data's fetch_expenses.py counting rules.
-     Quotations, products or any other Odoo area in the layer (later Requests, each with its own /<area>/ prefix and its own Odoo user and key).
-     A custom domain for the layer (workers.dev only for now).
-     A generic pass-through route to Odoo: every route allows a fixed list of Odoo models and methods only.

## Observations
-     Va Odoo layer naming and /expenses/ paths: worker/wrangler.json names the Worker 'odoo' and its D1 database 'ike-odoo'; the Worker answers the save, the day list, the categories and the retry at /expenses/entries, /expenses/entries?date=, /expenses/categories and /expenses/entries/<id>/retry, and no longer at the old unprefixed paths; OPTIONS still answers 204 on any path and the origin check still applies on every path; the page calls only the /expenses/ paths; every V2-V5 behaviour (save-first, PIN, origin, rate limit, idempotency, Odoo draft sync with retry, status read-back) is unchanged.
-     Vb Live-schema corrections: the Worker sends the bank-transfer payment method line as the configured id ODOO_PAYMENT_METHOD_LINE_ID (live: 2, 'Transfer', Bank journal, outbound) with no payment method line search, and an absent or non-positive-integer id leaves the entry 'Not sent' with a closed error code, never a crash; each created hr.expense carries total_amount_currency equal to total_amount and never price_unit; on a 'Not sent' entry, pressing Retry in the page calls the Worker's retry for that entry, the button is disabled while the call runs, and the row then shows the returned status ('Draft' on success, or 'Not sent' with the new next retry time), while a failed call keeps the row and shows a plain message.
-     Vc Live with the owner: the read-only live-Odoo checks confirm or correct each fact the fake Odoo assumed (total_amount writable, name as the mark, the bank-transfer payment method line, the 21 expensable products, how the receipt attaches, the state values, the 'id in' read, read access for the layer's key); the Worker is deployed on Cloudflare as 'odoo' with its 15-minute schedule and the page on GitHub Pages carries its URL; the owner records one real bank-transfer expense from a phone in under 30 seconds; the accountant finds it in Odoo as a draft hr.expense with the right category, amount and receipt without asking; after a person approves it in Odoo its chip shows 'Approved' within one schedule interval; the owner changes the PIN once in the Cloudflare dashboard, the new PIN saves and the old PIN is refused.

## Decisions
-     D1. Bank-transfer expenses only. No cash and no cash/transfer choice.
-     D2. Entry fields: category (dropdown), amount (MVR), optional receipt (camera or gallery), PIN. No 'who', no 'paid from', no 'paid to'.
-     D3. The PIN is one shared key that authorises saving. It does not identify a person.
-     D4. Every entry belongs to one default Odoo employee: Ahmed Rashad (hr.employee id 1).
-     D5. The app only creates a DRAFT hr.expense in Odoo. No submit, approve, post or pay from the app.
-     D6. No amount limit.
-     D7. Categories are Odoo expense categories: 23 exist; 'Expenses' and 'Mileage' are excluded; 'Salary' is included -> 21: Advertising & Marketing, Communication, Electricity, Fuel / Petrol, Gate Pass (Boat Delivery), Gifts, Internet, Meals, Medical Checkup (Visa), Salary, Shop Maintenance & Repairs, Shop Rent, Software Subscriptions, Staff Accommodation Rent, Stationery & Packing Supplies, Travel & Accommodation, Vehicle Maintenance, Visa & Work Permit, Warehouse Rent, Waste Disposal, Water.
-     D8. Reliable before instant: save first on the server side, sync to Odoo later, retry on failure, never lose an entry, never create a duplicate.
-     D9. A third tab 'Expenses' in the existing ike-sales page (static GitHub Pages, no build step, same look, plum accent, light + dark). Approved screens: design/expenses-tab/ (live at https://claude.ai/artifact/9cwfccMncJ7pWwr6twq98Z).
-     D10. Hosting: Cloudflare Worker + D1 for save-and-sync. The Odoo key is a Worker secret only, never in the page. The page is public, so the Worker checks the PIN and the page's origin.
-     D11. Read side exists: ike-data/data/expenses.json (approved/posted/in_payment/paid = confirmed; draft/submitted = pending; refused dropped; category list counts confirmed only; refreshed every 15 minutes). App drafts therefore show as pending, not in the total.
-     D12. Status chips: 'Waiting to send' (saved in the Worker, not yet in Odoo), 'Not sent' (last Odoo call failed; shows next retry time and a Retry button), 'Draft' (in Odoo as draft or submitted; pending, not counted), 'Approved' (approved, posted, in_payment or paid; counted), 'Refused' (refused in Odoo; never counted, stays in the list). The mapping follows the D11 counting rule, so a chip always agrees with the total. The design's 'Posted' is replaced by these words, its legend reads 'Waiting > Draft > Approved', and the Add sheet no longer says 'Posts straight to Odoo'.
-     D13. Read-back: on the same Cloudflare Cron Trigger that runs sync retries, every 15 minutes (the ike-data refresh interval), the Worker makes one read-only search_read on hr.expense for the Odoo ids of its entries that still show 'Draft' and updates their chips; an entry in a final chip (Approved or Refused) is not read again. The page never calls Odoo; chip and day total may disagree for up to about 15 minutes because the two schedules are independent.
-     D14. The owner sets and changes the PIN without a developer: it is a Worker secret changed in the Cloudflare dashboard. A new PIN works at once, the old one stops, saved entries are not affected. Wrong PINs are rate-limited. A leak is handled by the owner changing it.
-     D15. Everyone sees the day's full entry list (no names exist, so there is no 'own' list).
-     D16. The Odoo expense date is the Maldives (UTC+5) date on which the entry was saved, not the date it reached Odoo.
-     D17. The Worker is the ike Odoo layer: Worker name 'odoo' on the owner's workers.dev subdomain 'ike-mrh' ('ike' was taken), URL https://odoo.ike-mrh.workers.dev, D1 database 'ike-odoo'. Expense routes move under /expenses/: /expenses/entries, /expenses/categories, /expenses/entries/<id>/retry. OPTIONS stays 204 on any path and the origin check stays on every path. Each later Odoo area gets its own /<area>/ prefix and its own Odoo user and key; no route passes arbitrary Odoo calls through.
-     D18. Live-schema corrections from the read-only check of the live Odoo on 2026-10-07: no payment method line is named 'Bank Transfer MVR'; the company-paid bank-transfer line is id 2 'Transfer' (journal 6 Bank, outbound) and id 1 is also 'Transfer' (inbound), so a name search is ambiguous and the Worker sends the configured id ODOO_PAYMENT_METHOD_LINE_ID instead. total_amount and total_amount_currency are both writable and equal for MVR, the company currency; price_unit is readonly and computed, so it is never sent. The other assumed facts (name as the mark, the 21 products, receipt by res_model/res_id, the 7 state values, the 'id in' read shape, employee 1, payment_mode company_account) were confirmed. The page's Retry button (D12) calls the Worker's retry route.
