# Product brief

## Request
    Staff can record a bank-transfer expense from their phone in under 30 seconds, and the accountant sees it in Odoo without asking in WhatsApp. Context: MRH Investment (Maldives, shop + warehouse + wholesale, MVR). Different staff pay different expenses and the accountant asks in a WhatsApp group for each amount. Odoo (mrh-investment.odoo.com, saas-19.4) holds expenses as hr.expense, but 31 of 36 records have no receipt, 20 of 36 use the generic category 'Expenses', and some sit in draft, so totals are wrong. Staff have no Odoo logins and will not use Odoo screens. Jobs: Staff - record the transfer I just made, fast, on my phone, with the receipt if I have it. Accountant - see what was spent, by category and day, without asking. Owner/manager - keep bad entries out of the books (a person submits and approves drafts in Odoo).

## Outcomes
-     Staff record one bank-transfer expense from a phone in under 30 seconds, from opening the Expenses tab to seeing the entry in the day's list as saved.
-     The accountant finds each recorded expense in Odoo as a draft hr.expense with a specific category and, when one was given, its receipt, without asking in WhatsApp.
-     No entry is lost and none is duplicated: every entry the page shows as saved reaches Odoo exactly once, also when Odoo or the network fails.
-     Bad entries stay out of the books: app entries are Odoo drafts and count in the day total only after a person approves them in Odoo, and the page shows each entry's Odoo outcome (Approved or Refused) without anyone asking.
-     The Odoo key never reaches the public page, and only a holder of the shared PIN, from the ike-sales page, can save an entry.

## Scope

### In scope
-     A third pane 'Expenses' in the existing ike-sales page (index.html, static GitHub Pages, no build step), with the Add sheet and entry status chips from design/expenses-tab/, light and dark.
-     A Cloudflare Worker + D1 that checks PIN and origin, saves each entry (with its receipt) before anything else, serves the 21 categories and the day's entries with their sync status.
-     A sync from the Worker to Odoo that creates one draft hr.expense per saved entry, with retry and duplicate protection, and attaches the receipt.
-     A read-back on the Worker's schedule that updates each entry's chip from its Odoo state (Draft, Approved, Refused).
-     Reading the day's confirmed total, pending total and category list from ike-data/data/expenses.json.
-     Deploy, one live entry with the owner, and one PIN change by the owner.

### Out of scope
Applicability: applicable
Reason:     The request is about recording bank-transfer expenses and making them visible in Odoo; these items are separate jobs or later decisions.
-     Cash expenses, cash/transfer choice, till reconciliation.
-     Editing or deleting an entry from the page.
-     Submitting, approving, posting or paying from the page or the Worker.
-     A receipt requirement above an amount (decision 6: no limit now; revisit later).
-     Per-person PINs or any staff identity; per-device entry lists.
-     WhatsApp bots, multi-currency, reports beyond the existing dashboard.
-     Changes to ike-data's fetch_expenses.py counting rules.

## Observations
-     V1 Expenses pane with dummy data: ike-sales gains a third tab 'Expenses', reached by swipe and by the tab bar like Sales and Quotations, plum accent, ids and classes scoped to the pane; it shows the day's confirmed total, a separate pending line, the category list and the day's entries with chips 'Waiting to send', 'Not sent' (with next retry time and Retry), 'Draft', 'Approved' and 'Refused'; the Add sheet takes amount (MVR), one of the 21 categories, an optional receipt from camera or gallery, and the PIN; on a phone it matches design/expenses-tab/ in light and dark (with the D12 words); it makes no network call; an entry added on the sheet appears in the list as 'Waiting to send'; Sales and Quotations behave as before.
-     V2 Save-first service: a Cloudflare Worker with D1 answers 'saved' for an entry (amount, category, optional receipt, PIN, client entry id) only after the entry and its receipt are stored durably; it refuses a wrong PIN, a request from any origin other than the ike-sales page, an unknown category, and an amount that is not a positive MVR number, and it rate-limits wrong PINs; the same client entry id sent twice stores one entry, not two; it serves the 21 categories and the day's entries with their status; the PIN is a Worker secret, and a changed secret takes effect at once without touching saved entries.
-     V3 Pane on real data: the Expenses pane takes its 21 categories and the day's entries with status from the service, and the day's confirmed total, pending total and category list from ike-data/data/expenses.json; 'Post expense' on a phone saves through the service, closes the sheet and shows the entry as 'Waiting to send', inside the 30-second budget; a wrong PIN or an unreachable service keeps the typed entry in the sheet with a plain message, and the page never shows an entry as saved that the service did not confirm.
-     V4 Odoo sync: for each saved entry the Worker creates exactly one DRAFT hr.expense (employee Ahmed Rashad id 1, the chosen category, the amount in MVR, company-paid by bank transfer, dated per D16, the receipt attached when given, and a unique mark naming the entry) and never submits, approves, posts or pays it; a failed or unanswered Odoo call leaves the entry saved as 'Not sent' with its next retry time, it retries on schedule and on Retry, and a retry after a create whose answer was lost finds the existing expense by its mark instead of creating a second; once created the entry shows 'Draft' and appears as pending in expenses.json; the Odoo API key exists only as a Worker secret.
-     V5 Status read-back: every 15 minutes, on the same Worker schedule as the sync retries, the Worker reads the Odoo state of its entries that still show 'Draft' with one read-only call and sets each chip by D12: draft or submitted stays 'Draft', approved/posted/in_payment/paid becomes 'Approved', refused becomes 'Refused'; an entry in Approved or Refused is not read again; an Odoo failure during read-back changes no chip and is tried again on the next run; the Worker writes nothing to Odoo in this step.
-     V6 Live with the owner: the Worker is deployed on Cloudflare with its 15-minute schedule and the page on GitHub Pages; the owner records one real bank-transfer expense from a phone in under 30 seconds; the accountant finds it in Odoo as a draft hr.expense with the right category, amount and receipt without asking; after a person approves it in Odoo its chip shows 'Approved' within one schedule interval; the owner changes the PIN once in the Cloudflare dashboard, the new PIN saves and the old PIN is refused.

## Decisions
-     D1. Bank-transfer expenses only. No cash and no cash/transfer choice.
-     D2. Entry fields: category (dropdown), amount (MVR), optional receipt (camera or gallery), PIN. No 'who', no 'paid from', no 'paid to'.
-     D3. The PIN is one shared key that authorises saving. It does not identify a person.
-     D4. Every entry belongs to one default Odoo employee: Ahmed Rashad (hr.employee id 1).
-     D5. The app only creates a DRAFT hr.expense in Odoo. No submit, approve, post or pay from the app.
-     D6. No amount limit.
-     D7. Categories are Odoo expense categories: 23 exist; 'Expenses' and 'Mileage' are excluded; 'Salary' is included -> 21: Advertising & Marketing, Communication, Electricity, Fuel / Petrol, Gate Pass (Boat Delivery), Gifts, Internet, Meals, Medical Checkup (Visa), Salary, Shop Maintenance & Repairs, Shop Rent, Software Subscriptions, Staff Accommodation Rent, Stationery & Packing Supplies, Travel & Accommodation, Vehicle Maintenance, Visa & Work Permit, Warehouse Rent, Waste Disposal, Water.
-     D8. Reliable before instant: save first on the server side, sync to Odoo later, retry on failure, never lose an entry, never create a duplicate.
-     D9. A third tab 'Expenses' in the existing ike-sales page (static GitHub Pages, no build step, same look, plum accent, light + dark). Approved screens: design/expenses-tab/ (live at https://claude.ai/artifact/9cwfccMncJ7pWwr6twq98Z).
-     D10. Hosting: Cloudflare Worker + D1 for save-and-sync. The Odoo key is a Worker secret only, never in the page. The page is public, so the Worker checks the PIN and the page's origin.
-     D11. Read side exists: ike-data/data/expenses.json (approved/posted/in_payment/paid = confirmed; draft/submitted = pending; refused dropped; category list counts confirmed only; refreshed every 15 minutes). App drafts therefore show as pending, not in the total.
-     D12. Status chips: 'Waiting to send' (saved in the Worker, not yet in Odoo), 'Not sent' (last Odoo call failed; shows next retry time and a Retry button), 'Draft' (in Odoo as draft or submitted; pending, not counted), 'Approved' (approved, posted, in_payment or paid; counted), 'Refused' (refused in Odoo; never counted, stays in the list). The mapping follows the D11 counting rule, so a chip always agrees with the total. The design's 'Posted' is replaced by these words, its legend reads 'Waiting > Draft > Approved', and the Add sheet no longer says 'Posts straight to Odoo'.
-     D13. Read-back: on the same Cloudflare Cron Trigger that runs sync retries, every 15 minutes (the ike-data refresh interval), the Worker makes one read-only search_read on hr.expense for the Odoo ids of its entries that still show 'Draft' and updates their chips; an entry in a final chip (Approved or Refused) is not read again. The page never calls Odoo; chip and day total may disagree for up to about 15 minutes because the two schedules are independent.
-     D14. The owner sets and changes the PIN without a developer: it is a Worker secret changed in the Cloudflare dashboard. A new PIN works at once, the old one stops, saved entries are not affected. Wrong PINs are rate-limited. A leak is handled by the owner changing it.
-     D15. Everyone sees the day's full entry list (no names exist, so there is no 'own' list).
-     D16. The Odoo expense date is the Maldives (UTC+5) date on which the entry was saved, not the date it reached Odoo.
